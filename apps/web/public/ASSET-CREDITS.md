# Local demo assets

The existing illustrative pizza photography is served from this application and bundled in the Flutter client, so visitors do not contact the image host. These images do not represent actual menu inventory.

Source image IDs on Unsplash:

- `photo-1579751626657-72bc17010498`
- `photo-1571407970349-bc81e7e96d47`

Source URLs use `https://images.unsplash.com/<image-id>`. Downloading, copying, and distributing these images is permitted by the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license), checked 2026-10-24. They are used here as illustrations, not resold as stock images.

DM Sans and Playfair Display are served locally. Their original copyright notices and SIL Open Font License terms are included beside the font files in `fonts/dmsans-OFL.txt` and `fonts/playfairdisplay-OFL.txt`. Font files came from Google Fonts; license sources are the corresponding `ofl/dmsans` and `ofl/playfairdisplay` directories in the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts).
